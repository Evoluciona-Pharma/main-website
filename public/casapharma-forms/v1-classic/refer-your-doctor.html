Refer Your Doctor

Casa Pharma RX works exclusively with licensed physicians. As a licensed 503A sterile compounding pharmacy, we require a valid prescription for all compounded medications.

If your healthcare provider isn't familiar with Casa Pharma RX, use the form below to refer your doctor. We'll provide information about our pharmacy, compounded medications, and the prescribing process so they can determine whether our services are appropriate for your care.

How it works:

  • Complete the referral form with your doctor's contact information.
  • We'll send your physician information about Casa Pharma RX and our compounded medications.
  • Your doctor will determine whether treatment through Casa Pharma RX is appropriate for your care.
  • You'll receive a confirmation copy of your referral for your records.
